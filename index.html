Privacy Policy

Look Away · Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how the Look Away Android app (package name com.lookaway.eyebreaks) handles information. Look Away provides eye-break reminders, optional reminder schedules, a manually updated water log, and a banner ad. It does not require an account or sign-in.

In brief: Reminder settings, reminder counts, and the water amount you log are stored locally on your device. The app developer does not receive them. The Google Mobile Ads SDK does send certain device and ad-use information to Google to provide and measure ads and help prevent fraud.

Information stored on your device

Look Away stores the settings needed to run the app: whether reminders are on or paused, the reminder interval and end time, selected reminder days and schedule times, a daily reminder count, and the water amount you manually log for the current day. This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is not sent to the developer.

The app does not access contacts, photos, microphone, precise location, or health sensors. The water tracker does not read water intake automatically; it records only the amount you choose to add. Android notifications are used to deliver reminders, and a boot-completed event lets an active reminder schedule resume after the device restarts. Android Auto Backup is disabled for the app.

Local settings remain on the device until you change them, clear Look Away's app storage, or uninstall the app. The tracker shows the current day's total rather than a historical log; a new day's entry replaces the previous stored total when you log water again.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display a banner advertisement. The SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • ad and app interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, crashes or hangs, and energy use; and
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and App Set ID, where available.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, ad measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, and the ad configuration, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Google processes ad data under its own policies and may use service providers and advertising partners to deliver or measure ads. Ad-related data is transmitted using encryption in transit, as described by Google.

Look Away uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent or privacy messages when required. If an ad privacy options control is shown in the app, you can use it to review or change the choices available for your region. You can also manage your Android advertising ID and Google ad personalization settings through your device and Google account.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Google Play.

Sharing

The developer does not sell or receive the local reminder settings or water log. The advertising information described above is transmitted to Google and may be processed by Google's advertising service providers and partners as needed to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. The app does not include a separate analytics or account service.

Security and retention

Local app settings are kept in Android's private app storage. The app does not operate a server or keep a developer-side copy of the reminder or water information. Google Mobile Ads data is handled and retained under Google's applicable privacy and retention practices; see Google's policies linked above for details.

Your choices and deleting information

  • You can turn reminders off or change your reminder and water-log settings in the app.
  • You can clear the app's storage or uninstall Look Away to remove its locally stored settings and water log.
  • You can deny notification permission; reminders then may not appear.
  • You can review ad privacy choices in the app when the ad privacy options control is available, and manage advertising ID or Google ad-personalization settings using Android and Google controls.

Because Look Away has no user account and the developer does not receive the local app data, there is no developer-held account data to request deletion of. Requests about data handled by Google can be made using Google's privacy controls and processes.

Children

Look Away does not ask users for their name, email address, date of birth, or other account details, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Google Mobile Ads may process the device and ad-related information described above. If the app is offered to children or is declared as child-directed in Google Play, the developer must update the app's audience and advertising configuration to meet the applicable Google Play and AdMob requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the policy URL shown in the app and on the Google Play listing. The date at the top indicates when this page was most recently updated.
